# Ashlr.ai — 90-Day Upwork Revenue Ramp Playbook
## $0 to $20K-$50K/month

**Starting position:** $40K/month off-Upwork retail AI engagement, production products (AO, TourVault, Triage), 100 connects, $75/hr rate set, identity verification pending.

---

## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Weeks 1-2)
### Goal: First 2-3 contracts, first 5-star reviews

### Day 1-3: Profile & Identity

**BLOCKER: Complete identity verification immediately.** You cannot submit proposals or appear in search results until this clears. Expedite this above all else.

**Profile optimization checklist:**
- [ ] Profile title: "AI Software Engineering | Custom LLM Integration, Automation & Analytics" (specific, not generic)
- [ ] Overview: Lead with the $40K/month retail AI engagement as social proof. Mention 2,700+ tests on AO, production SaaS products. Clients buy confidence, not resumes
- [ ] Portfolio: Add 3-4 case studies with screenshots — AO dashboard, TourVault analytics, Triage email classification. Show working software, not wireframes
- [ ] Skills tags: AI Development, Python, LLM Integration, Machine Learning, API Development, Full Stack Development, Automation, Data Analytics
- [ ] Profile photo: Professional headshot (not logo — clients hire people)
- [ ] Set availability to "More than 30 hrs/week"
- [ ] Add employment history and education to hit 100% profile completion (required for Rising Talent badge)

### Day 3-14: First Contracts Strategy

**What to apply for (in priority order):**

1. **Small, well-defined AI projects ($500-$2,000 fixed price)** — "Build me a chatbot," "integrate OpenAI API into my app," "create a Python automation script." These are your review-acquisition vehicles. They take 1-3 days and get you to 5-star reviews fast.

2. **Avoid:**
   - Massive vague projects ("Build me an AI platform") — these attract 50+ proposals and go nowhere
   - $5-$15/hr budget clients — they will drain you and leave bad reviews over nothing
   - Contests or spec work

3. **Target clients who are also new to Upwork** — they face the same cold-start problem you do. A first-time Upwork buyer is more open to hiring someone without reviews. Filter for "No hires" or "1st project posted" in the client info sidebar.

**Proposal volume and connects strategy:**
- Submit 3-5 targeted proposals per day (15-25/week)
- Spend 4-6 connects per proposal on jobs under $1K (Upwork's standard cost)
- Spend 6-8 connects on jobs $1K-$5K
- Do NOT boost proposals yet — you have no review history to back up the visibility. Save connects for volume.
- 100 connects = roughly 15-20 proposals. Buy another 80-100 connects ($0.15/each = $15) on day 1.
- Budget: $30-$50/month on connects in Month 1

**Pricing strategy for Weeks 1-2:**

This is the most important tactical decision. Here is the recommendation:

| Approach | Rate | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| Fixed-price proposals | $500-$2,000 | Quote a fixed price on small projects. This avoids the hourly rate comparison entirely. |
| If hourly is required | $50-$65/hr | Not $75. With zero reviews, $75/hr gets filtered out. Drop to $50-$65 for the first 3-4 contracts. You are buying reviews, not maximizing revenue. |
| Never go below | $40/hr | Below this attracts budget clients who leave problems, not reviews |

**The math:** 3 small contracts at $1,000 each = $3,000 gross, $2,700 after Upwork's 10% fee. You earn $2,700 AND you earn 3 five-star reviews. That is the real value.

### Proposal Template (Adapt, Never Copy-Paste)

```
Hi [Client Name],

[1 sentence showing you read their job post — reference a specific detail they mentioned]

I've built production AI systems that are currently in use — including an agent orchestration platform with 2,700+ tests and a golf analytics SaaS processing real tournament data. [Swap in whichever product is most relevant to THEIR need]

For your project, here's what I'd do:
- [Specific step 1 — shows you understand the technical approach]
- [Specific step 2]
- [Deliverable and timeline]

I can start [today/tomorrow] and deliver a working prototype within [X days].

Happy to jump on a quick call to walk through the approach.

— Mason, Ashlr.ai
```

**Key proposal rules:**
- 150-250 words max. Clients skim.
- First sentence MUST reference something specific from their job post
- Include a concrete deliverable and timeline
- End with a call to action (call, prototype, test task)
- Submit within 1-4 hours of the job posting. Speed is the single highest-correlation factor with getting hired.

### Upwork Fee Handling

Upwork charges a flat 10% service fee on all earnings. Do NOT try to hide this or pass it to the client. Build it into your pricing:

| You want to net | Quote the client |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| $50/hr | $56/hr |
| $65/hr | $72/hr |
| $75/hr | $83/hr |
| $100/hr | $111/hr |
| $1,000 fixed | $1,111 fixed |

Just bake the 10% into every quote. Never mention the fee to clients.

## PHASE 2: MOMENTUM (Weeks 3-4)
### Goal: Rising Talent badge, 5+ reviews, $3K-$5K in earnings

### Converting First Clients to 5-Star Reviews

This is where most agencies fail. The review is more valuable than the payment on your first 5 contracts.

**Over-delivery protocol:**
1. Respond to all messages within 1 hour during business hours
2. Deliver 1 day early on every contract
3. Add one small bonus feature they didn't ask for ("I also added error logging and a health check endpoint — no extra charge")
4. Send a Loom video walking through the deliverable
5. At project end, send this exact message:

> "Really enjoyed working on this. If you're happy with the work, I'd appreciate a review — it helps a lot as I'm building my Upwork presence. Happy to make any final tweaks before you close the contract."

**Never ask for "5 stars" explicitly** — that is against Upwork TOS. But the above phrasing, combined with over-delivery, gets 5 stars 90%+ of the time.

### Rising Talent Badge

Rising Talent is awarded automatically when you meet these criteria:
- 100% complete profile
- High-quality proposals submitted
- Selected for and completed jobs with positive feedback
- JSS (Job Success Score) of 90%+ if you have one (you can get the badge before earning a JSS)

**Timeline:** With 2-3 completed contracts and good feedback, expect the badge within 2-4 weeks. You get a 30 Connects bonus when awarded.

**Critical:** Do NOT let any contract go sideways in this period. One bad review when you have 2-3 total reviews is catastrophic. If a client is difficult, offer a full refund and mutual contract cancellation (no review recorded) rather than risk a bad review.

### When to Raise Rates

| Milestone | Hourly Rate | Fixed-Price Multiplier |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| 0 reviews | $50-$65/hr | 1.0x |
| 3 reviews, all 5-star | $65-$75/hr | 1.2x |
| 5+ reviews, Rising Talent | $75-$85/hr | 1.3x |
| Top Rated (Month 3+) | $100-$125/hr | 1.5x |
| Top Rated Plus | $125-$175/hr | 2.0x |

Raise rates by $10-15/hr after each milestone. Never jump more than $20 at once.

### Week 3-4 Job Targeting

Start mixing in larger projects alongside quick wins:
- Continue 1-2 small projects/week for review volume
- Start applying to $3,000-$8,000 projects
- Target: "Build an AI-powered [X]" or "Integrate LLM into existing [Y] app"
- Begin applying to jobs that explicitly mention ongoing/retainer needs

## PHASE 3: SCALING (Month 2, Weeks 5-8)
### Goal: $10K-$15K in monthly billings, repeat clients, agency credibility

### Moving to $10K+ Contracts

With 5+ five-star reviews and Rising Talent badge, you qualify for the jobs that actually pay. Shift proposal targeting:

| Job Type | Budget | Your Proposal Angle |
|----------|--------|-------------------|
| AI/ML integration | $5K-$15K | "We built AO, a production orchestration platform with 2,700+ tests. Here's exactly how we'd approach your integration." |
| Custom software builds | $10K-$25K | "We ship production software — our team delivered a $40K/month retail AI system. We can have a prototype in your hands in 2 weeks." |
| Ongoing AI consulting | $5K-$10K/mo | "We provide fractional AI engineering — think of us as your AI team without the hiring overhead." |

### Building Repeat Client Relationships

Repeat clients are the engine of Upwork revenue. They skip the proposal competition entirely.

**Tactics:**
1. At end of every contract, ask: "Do you have any other projects coming up? Happy to discuss."
2. Send a follow-up message 2-3 weeks after contract closes: "Hey [Name], wanted to check in — any new projects on the horizon? We just shipped [recent capability] that might be relevant."
3. Offer a "retainer discount" — 10% off hourly for clients who commit to 20+ hrs/week ongoing
4. Create a private Upwork "catalog listing" (Project Catalog) for ongoing AI consulting — repeat clients can re-hire with one click

### Portfolio Optimization

By now you have real Upwork work to showcase. Update your profile:
- Replace pre-Upwork portfolio items with Upwork project results (with client permission)
- Add specific metrics: "Reduced processing time by 60%," "Integrated 3 LLM providers in 2 weeks"
- Get at least one client testimonial quote for your overview

### Connects Strategy Month 2

- Budget: $40-$60/month on connects
- Proposal volume: 10-15/week (more selective now)
- Start using boosts selectively: Only on jobs $5K+ that are an exact fit for your niche. Spend 10-20 extra connects on these.
- Target win rate: 8-12% (industry benchmark for AI/data agencies)

## PHASE 4: DOMINATION (Month 3, Weeks 9-12)
### Goal: Top Rated badge, $20K-$30K/month, enterprise pipeline

### Top Rated Badge Requirements

You need ALL of these:
- **JSS 90%+** for at least 13 of the last 16 weeks
- **$1,000+ in trailing 12-month earnings** (you will have this)
- **Active on platform** in last 90 days (proposals, invitations, or earnings)
- **Account in good standing** — no holds, no TOS violations
- **For agency specifically:** 12-month agency earnings of at least $10,000

**Timeline reality check:** Top Rated typically takes 16+ weeks of consistent activity. At month 3, you may be close but not there yet. If you hit all metrics by week 10-12, you could see the badge by week 14-16. The badge is evaluated every 2 weeks.

**Top Rated benefits:**
- Badge on profile and proposals (massive trust signal)
- Access to Top Rated-only job invitations
- "Top Rated perk" — ability to remove one bad review per quarter
- Higher search ranking

### Winning Enterprise Clients

Enterprise clients on Upwork typically:
- Post jobs with $10K-$50K+ budgets
- Require agency profiles (not individual freelancers)
- Look for Top Rated or Top Rated Plus badges
- Want to see team depth, not just one person

**How to position for enterprise:**
1. Set up an Upwork Agency profile (separate from individual) with team members listed
2. Create a Project Catalog listing for "AI Consulting & Custom Development" — this is a fixed-price service listing that clients can buy directly
3. Apply to Enterprise-tagged jobs with emphasis on team capability and production track record
4. Offer a paid discovery phase: "We do a $2,500 two-week discovery sprint to map your AI opportunities and deliver a technical roadmap"

### Project Catalog Service Listing

Project Catalog lets you list productized services that clients can purchase directly. Set up:

| Listing | Price | Description |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| AI Integration Assessment | $2,500 | 2-week discovery sprint. Audit your tech stack, identify AI opportunities, deliver implementation roadmap. |
| Custom AI Agent Build | $8,000-$15,000 | Production-ready AI agent tailored to your workflow. Includes testing, deployment, 30 days support. |
| LLM-Powered Feature Development | $5,000-$10,000 | Add AI capabilities to your existing application. RAG, embeddings, classification, generation. |

## SPECIAL TOPIC: Selling AO ($9,500) and TourVault ($12,000) on Upwork

### Short Answer: Not Directly, But Yes Indirectly

Upwork is a services marketplace, not a product marketplace. Posting a proposal that says "buy my finished product for $9,500" will not work and may violate TOS if positioned as a product sale rather than a service engagement.

**What DOES work:**

1. **"Implementation and Customization" framing:** When you see a job post like "We need an AI agent system" or "We need a golf analytics platform," propose AO or TourVault as the starting point:

   > "We've already built a production agent orchestration platform (2,700+ tests, desktop app, full dashboard). Rather than building from scratch, I'd customize and deploy our existing platform for your needs. This saves you 3-4 months of development time."

   Price this as a service engagement ($8,000-$15,000) that happens to include your software. The client is paying for customization, deployment, training, and support — the software is the delivery vehicle.

2. **White-label licensing through service contracts:** Structure the deal as:
   - $5,000 implementation/customization (service — goes through Upwork)
   - $4,500 annual license (can be invoiced separately after the relationship is established off-Upwork, per Upwork's 2-year exclusivity rule)

3. **Use products as portfolio proof, not as the sale itself.** The fact that you built AO and TourVault is your competitive advantage in proposals. It proves you can ship. Let it sell your services, which then happen to include your products.

### Upwork's Exclusivity Rule

Important: Once you connect with a client through Upwork, you must use Upwork for payments for 2 years (or pay a conversion fee). Plan your product licensing revenue accordingly — either run it through Upwork (losing 10%) or structure it as a separate business relationship after the exclusivity period.

## RISK FACTORS AND MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Identity verification delay | BLOCKS everything | Contact Upwork support daily until resolved. Have all docs ready. |
| First review is bad | Devastating at this stage | Over-deliver. Offer refund + mutual cancellation if ANY friction. |
| Slow start (no hires in 2 weeks) | Morale and momentum | Drop rate to $40-$50/hr temporarily. Apply to MORE small jobs. Quantity over quality at this stage. |
| Difficult client | Could tank JSS | Screen clients: check their review history, payment verification, hire rate. Avoid clients with <4.5 stars. |
| Running out of connects | Can't submit proposals | Budget $50/month minimum. Connects are the cheapest investment on the platform. |
| Upwork's 2-year exclusivity | Limits off-platform revenue | Structure product licensing carefully. Use Upwork for services, separate entity for product sales if needed. |

## BOTTOM LINE

The fastest path from $0 to $20K/month on Upwork:

1. **Weeks 1-2:** Buy reviews with small, fast projects at a lower rate. Speed and over-delivery are everything.
2. **Weeks 3-4:** Leverage reviews + Rising Talent to access better jobs. Start mixing in $3K-$8K projects.
3. **Month 2:** Land 1-2 retainer clients and 1-2 large fixed-price contracts. This is where revenue compounds.
4. **Month 3:** Use Top Rated status + portfolio to win enterprise deals. Project Catalog generates inbound. Repeat clients reduce proposal effort.

The agency's existing $40K/month off-Upwork engagement and production products (AO, TourVault, Triage) are massive advantages. Most new Upwork agencies have nothing to show. Ashlr has shipping proof. Lead with that in every single proposal.
